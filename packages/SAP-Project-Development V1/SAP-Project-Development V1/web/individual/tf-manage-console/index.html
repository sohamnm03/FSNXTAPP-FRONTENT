BL Compliance Checklist
1. Port of loading, Port of Discharge, Commodity, shipment date for the selected BL shall match with the requirements of the Master Contract for which the BL is being selected.
2. Load Port, Discharge Port, Origin of goods shall not be from any of Countries in OFAC list.
3. Scanned copies of OBL shall be used for TSF Merchanting book. No Seaway Bils/ Draft BL's are accepted for FX invoicing.
4. Consignee should be 'To Order'. If Consignee is "third party BL" or "Bank", such BL shall not be acceptable unless approved otherwise. If approved, the bank on face of BL, and Bank for TSF transaction (FX+ Document Submission) shall be different. In no case, the BL can be used for FX transaction/ Document Submission at the same bank as that appearing on BL.
5. If the BL has LC details, the bank who is handling the BU documentation shouldn't be same as that of TSF.
6. If BU is routing documents through Bank on Buy or Sell Leg, TSF cannot use these BL for invoicing the FX transaction with these banks.
7. In other words, there cannot be an overlap between BU banks and TSF banks for same BL.
8. Invoice shall be created only on "Current market price" (+/- 5%) on the date of TSF invoicing. Any deviation shall be approved as per Trade flow Policy.
